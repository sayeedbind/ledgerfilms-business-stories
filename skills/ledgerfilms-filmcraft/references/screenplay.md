# Screenplay — the two-column AV script

A documentary screenplay is not an essay with pictures. It is a film written in two columns that are composed simultaneously.

## Format (one row per shot)
| # | VISUAL (what the viewer sees, in order) | AUDIO (narration line over this shot) |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | ECU on a phone screen in darkness: a line of small orange dots — a comment thread — scrolling fast, then stopping dead. Locked-off, slight handheld tremor. | “On Friday, Reddit did something it has done a hundred times. It reported a record quarter.” |

## Rules
1. **One idea per shot.** A narration sentence that contains two ideas gets two shots (or is split). A shot's action must complete inside its narration line.
2. **Concrete nouns win.** The line names things the camera can show: a door, a counter, a queue, a key, an empty chair. Abstract claims (“trust eroded”) are rewritten into visible events (“the queue outside the door is gone; the man locks up at noon”) or carried by a planned graphic.
3. **Show the mechanism, don't describe it twice.** If narration explains a process, the picture performs it step by step in the same order the words name the steps.
4. **Picture leads by half a beat.** The visual for an idea starts a fraction before the line names it, so eye and ear land together.
5. **Cause → effect on screen.** Shot N shows the cause; shot N+1 shows its visible consequence. Chains of these are what make it a movie instead of a slideshow.
6. **Write the turn visually.** The story's reversal must be a visible change in a place/character/prop the audience already knows (same door, now chained) — not a new generic location.
7. **Dialogue-free but character-led.** Faceless does not mean person-less: hands, silhouettes, backs, reflections. A recurring character with a goal (get through the door, keep the shop open) carries the spine.
8. **Narration discipline:** short sentences, one clause of new information each, written to the timing of the shots (about 2.2–2.5 words per second). No sentence may exist only to fill time between pictures.
9. **Every line auditable:** in review, each AUDIO cell must answer “where exactly do I see this?” pointing at its VISUAL cell. No answer = rewrite.

## Performance marks (directing the voice)
The AUDIO column is a performance script, not just text:
- `/` = a deliberate short pause (beat before a reveal or after a number).
- **Bold** = the one emphasised word in the sentence; at most one per line — emphasis everywhere is emphasis nowhere.
- The pack carries a **pronunciation list**: every proper name, company and number read aloud is checked against the TTS once; anything misread is respelled inside the spoken text (e.g. “Huff-man” if needed) with the correct spelling noted beside it. Mispronunciations are fixed at the chunk and re-generated, never left in.
- Numbers are written to be spoken (“twenty-nine percent”, “eight hundred ninety-five million dollars”) so the voice and the on-screen graphic say the same thing at the same moment.

## Worked micro-example (Reddit rebuild style)
- VISUAL: WS, dawn, a vast forum hall of glowing doorways; crowds stream through one huge open door. Slow crane down. AUDIO: “For twenty years, Reddit had two doors.”
- VISUAL: MS tracking shot following one silhouette through the huge door as it passes a glowing signpost (no readable text). AUDIO: “Most people never used the front door. They arrived from somewhere else —"
- VISUAL: ECU insert: a search bar shape of light; a finger taps; the huge door swings wider. AUDIO: “— typed a question into Google, and were handed straight in.”
The words and the pictures are the same event told twice, in sync.
