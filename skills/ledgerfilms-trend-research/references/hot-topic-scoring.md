# Hot-topic scoring — from signal to a pick you can defend

Score every candidate that survives the niche filter. Each factor 1–5, from the
**recorded evidence** — if you can't point to a link for a score, it's a 2 at best.

| # | Factor | 5 looks like | 1 looks like |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Trend velocity** | Rising/Breakout on Trends, posts & searches accelerating in the last 7 d | Flat or fading; the spike was last month |
| 2 | **Demand vs. supply** | Heavy autocomplete + search volume; existing videos weak, old, or from small channels | Every big channel covered it this week, well |
| 3 | **Pillar & mechanism fit** | Squarely in a pillar; the money mechanism is hidden, surprising, explainable in one line | Tangential to business; no mechanism, just events |
| 4 | **Hook strength** | A number, contradiction or moment that hooks in 3 seconds and re-hooks at 90 s | Needs a paragraph of setup before it's interesting |
| 5 | **Advertiser fit** | Finance/business/SaaS/productivity audience intent (the channel's reason for existing) | Low-intent curiosity with no advertiser behind it |
| 6 | **Evergreen tail** | Still searchable and true in 12 months; trend is the entry, story is the asset | Dead the week the news cycle moves on |
| 7 | **Production feasibility** | Visualisable in cinematic AI art; clear era/locations; bible builds cleanly | Needs real footage, charts on screen, or living-person likeness risk |
| 8 | **Shorts potential** | At least 2 standalone 45–60 s hooks inside it, not just cut-downs | One long argument that can't be split |
| 9 | **Legal safety** | Historical, documented, or company-level (no personal misconduct claims) | Rests on allegations against living people / active litigation claims |
| 10 | **Cadence fit** | Window fits the weekly slot (long-form Tue/Sun; Shorts worked a week ahead), or it's a valid quick Short | Window shuts in 48 h and only a long-form would do it justice |

**Total /50.** House thresholds:

- **40+** — slate it; ★ pick normally comes from here.
- **32–39** — slate it as a Short or a backup; long-form only with a strong evergreen tail (factor 6 ≥ 4).
- **Below 32** — drop it, and write down why (topic bank learns from rejections).

Tie-break, in order: velocity → demand vs. supply → advertiser fit.

## The publish-by window (state it for every slate item)

- **News-driven** (earnings, bankruptcy, ruling): publish within **3–7 days** of the
  event or don't ride it — do the evergreen version later instead.
- **Spike-driven** (Trends/Reddit surge, no single event): within **7–14 days**,
  while the related queries are still rising.
- **Calendar-driven** (anniversary, scheduled earnings/launch): publish **on or just
  before** the date — this is the only window you can safely build a week ahead for.
- **Evergreen-with-a-hook**: no deadline; the trend is a bonus, schedule it normally.

## What the slate must show (per candidate)

Working title (formula from `../../../docs/titles-thumbnails-metadata.md`) · pillar ·
the mechanism in one line · **why now**, with dated evidence links · velocity note
(rising/flat + timeframe + geo) · score breakdown + total · publish-by window ·
long-form, Short, or both · the 2–3 search phrases viewers are already using ·
legal note if ⚖️. Use `../../../templates/trend-brief.md`.

If **nothing** clears the filter on scan day, say so plainly, show the best near-miss
and why it failed, and fall back to the highest-scored evergreen in
`../../../production/topic-bank.md`. Never promote a weak trend just to have a trend.
