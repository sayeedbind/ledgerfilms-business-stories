# Fact-check QC (acceptance gate)

Adapted from the stickman agent's acceptance checklist, extended with LedgerFilms' fact rules.
ALL boxes must pass — or the failure is disclosed to the user with the export.

**Facts**
- [ ] Every load-bearing figure/date/quote has 2 sources in the brief, ≥1 primary where possible
- [ ] Every figure is narrated/shown with its year; currency stated
- [ ] Wrongdoing language matches the evidence ladder (convicted/charged/found/reported)
- [ ] No motive claims about living people without a source; no financial advice anywhere
- [ ] Captions and overlays match the brief's numbers exactly

**Bible & picture**
- [ ] Founder identity consistent in every clip (master used, never replaced)
- [ ] Era audit passed — no anachronistic tech, clothing, vehicles, signage
- [ ] Location layouts/counts stable; no mirrored sets; no extra/missing key subjects
- [ ] No model-rendered text, logos, charts or numbers in any scene
- [ ] Style line present in every prompt; grade consistent across clips

**Sound**
- [ ] **Every clip generated WITH its own voiceover** (dialog voiceover option, locked channel voice + settings) — no separate narration track exists
- [ ] Each clip's voiceover checked: present, complete, correct line, no truncation; first clip's voiceover starts at frame 1 (long-form) with no gap
- [ ] **Single voice per clip** — video prompts were visuals-only (no line/quoted speech in them); every clip's **tail listened to** for doubled audio; Video Only (No Sound) was OFF throughout
- [ ] Clip production sound low under the clip's voiceover (clips generated WITH described sound, never silent); music only the assembly track, ducked
- [ ] No invented dialogue from any character

**Edit & export**
- [ ] Clips in planned order, no duplicates, Auto Align done, dead-air trim passed at 1×
- [ ] **COVERAGE GATE (blocking): every planned beat has its clip, in order; every
      clip carries its checked voiceover; summed measured clip total ___ s lands in
      the format's runtime range. If this fails: STOP, no export — generate the
      missing/failed clips with their voiceovers (PLAYBOOK §6/§9)**
- [ ] Runtime: long-form 9–11 min from narration · Short 45–60 s
- [ ] **Context gate passed** (filmcraft): any 5 lines muted — WHAT / WHERE in the system / HOW it works answerable from the picture alone
- [ ] Export verified in My Videos at High/FullHD, correct ratio, downloaded/archived
- [ ] **Export itself verified** (not just its existence): played start/middle/end — picture, narration, sync, **closing line present**; project was ≤600 s or exported in parts and spliced (600 s cap); **head frame sampled on every clip** (no black/frozen openers); **contact sheet scanned** — no repeated visuals across clips
- [ ] Upload pack delivered: 3 titles (pick marked), description + sources + disclaimer,
      chapters, tags, pinned comment, thumbnail brief
