# Pacing & trim

From the mini-dramas playbook (fast-cut discipline), slowed one notch for documentary:

## Long-form
- Visual beat every **~3–5 s** inside a clip; a generation (multi-shot clip) carries
  **one voiceover line**, with 2–3 internal hard-cut shots whose timings are
  proportioned to the line's words (DURATION RULE: the clip lasts as long as its
  voiceover runs — plan ~70 generations per 10 minutes at ~20 words per line, with
  15–20% motif/B-roll reuse).
- **Measured coverage rules everything (PLAYBOOK §6/§9):** every clip is generated
  **with its own voiceover**, so its measured duration IS its narration beat's
  duration — the scene follows the voiceover by construction. Keep a running total
  of measured clip seconds while generating; generations needed = number of beats
  + ~10%.
- **Coverage failure is blocking:** a missing beat, or a clip whose voiceover is
  silent / truncated / wrong-voice / wrong-line, means do not export — regenerate
  that clip (with its voiceover) and reassemble. Never freeze, loop, slow or
  black-pad to fill.
- No visual may sit unchanged for >12 s except a deliberate "let it land" moment after the climax.
- A re-hook (new question / bigger number / reversal) every 90–120 s of narration.
- Cold open ≤30 s; no logos/intros before the hook.

## Shorts
- ~3 s per shot, 10–14 clips. First frame mid-action, never a static establishing shot.
- Reveal at ~70% (≈35–42 s). Last line loops or points to the long-form.

## The dead-air trim (both formats)
1. Auto Align Clips first.
2. At ~40 px/s timeline zoom, walk the timeline: **Cut + Delete** every frozen frame run,
   silent head and silent tail; close each gap as you go.
3. Shorts are stricter: no shot outlives its narration line.
4. Overrun rule: cut whole beats (picture + their voiceover together). Never trim
   through a spoken line and never cut a clip's voiceover off mid-line.
5. Final check at 1× speed, sound on: any 1-second stretch where nothing moves AND
   nothing is said is a trim you missed — unless it is a deliberate silent beat.
