# Pacing & trim

From the mini-dramas playbook (fast-cut discipline), slowed one notch for documentary:

## Long-form
- Visual beat every **~3–5 s**; a generation (multi-shot clip) covers 8–12 s with 2–3
  internal hard-cut shots. ~60–75 generations per 10 minutes, with 15–20% motif/B-roll reuse.
- **Measured coverage rules everything (PLAYBOOK §6/§9):** VideoExpress clips are
  generated at 8–10 s each; a route that returns ~5 s clips halves your coverage, so
  re-count at the measured length. Generations needed = ⌈ narration seconds ÷ 9 ⌉
  + ~10%. Keep a running total of measured clip seconds while generating.
- **Underrun is blocking:** if the assembled video track totals less than the
  narration − 2 s, do not export — generate additional B-roll/alternate angles for
  the short beats and reassemble. Never freeze, loop, slow or black-pad to fill.
- No visual may sit unchanged for >12 s except a deliberate "let it land" moment after the climax.
- A re-hook (new question / bigger number / reversal) every 90–120 s of narration.
- Cold open ≤30 s; no logos/intros before the hook.

## Shorts
- ~3 s per shot, 10–14 clips. First frame mid-action, never a static establishing shot.
- Reveal at ~70% (≈35–42 s). Last line loops or points to the long-form.

## The dead-air trim (both formats)
1. Auto Align Clips first.
2. At ~40 px/s timeline zoom, walk the timeline: **Cut + Delete** every frozen frame run,
   silent head and silent tail; close each gap as you go.
3. Shorts are stricter: no shot outlives its narration line.
4. Overrun rule: only the *video* is cut at the narration's right edge — playhead at the
   audio edge, select last video clip, Cut, delete the excess right-hand chunk.
5. Final check at 1× speed, sound on: any 1-second stretch where nothing moves AND
   nothing is said is a trim you missed.
