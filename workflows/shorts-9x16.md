# Workflow — Short (9:16, 45–60 s)

Input: one surprising fact / one mechanism. Output: exported 1080×1920 MP4 + title + pinned comment.
A Short is a complete story, not a trailer — except standalone teasers, which still must
deliver their own payoff before teasing the long-form.

1. **One fact check** → the single load-bearing claim gets the two-source treatment in a
   mini research brief. If it can't be verified, pick another hook — Shorts are where
   sloppy claims spread fastest.
2. **Script** → `templates/script-short.md`, 115–150 words:
   - 0–3 s: hook — the number or contradiction, spoken over a mid-action visual.
   - 3–35 s: setup + escalation, one beat per line, ~3 s per shot.
   - ~35–42 s (≈70%): **the reveal** — the hidden mechanism.
   - Final line: loop to line 1, or "the full story" pointer for teasers/cut-downs.
3. **Bible** → reuse the channel bible; if the Short belongs to a long-form, reuse that
   video's bible verbatim. Frame subject centre-safe for 9:16 — nothing important in the
   top/bottom 15% (UI zones).
4. **Prompt pack** → 10–14 clips. Same multishot format, same settings discipline:
   ratio **9:16 checked in the dialog**, enhance OFF, gallery OFF, no music.
5. **Narrate** → same channel narrator, same settings as the long-form. Lock it.
6. **Assemble & trim** → in order, Auto Align, then the strict trim: *no shot outlives
   its narration line*. Total must land 45–60 s from the narration, not from cutting audio.
7. **Captions** → burned-in or platform captions checked line by line — Shorts are watched
   muted; wrong captions = wrong facts. Numbers in captions must match the brief exactly.
8. **Export** → High / FullHD 1080×1920 MP4, verify, download. Name: `SH-YYYYMMDD-topic-slug`.
9. **Publish pack** → hook as the title, `#shorts`, pinned comment linking the related
   long-form (or teasing next week's), post 6–9 PM ET on its scheduled day.
