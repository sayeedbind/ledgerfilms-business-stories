# Workflow — Repurpose: 1 long-form → the week's Shorts

Research once, publish five times. Each week's long-form yields **2 cut-down Shorts**;
the other **2 Shorts are standalone** hooks researched at brief level only.

## Cut-downs (from this week's long-form)
Pick the two moments with a self-contained reveal:
- **Short A — "The Mechanism":** how the money actually worked, in 60 s.
- **Short B — "The Fall / The Peak":** the turning-point decision and its consequence.
Method — **re-frame, don't crop:**
1. Pull those script sections; rewrite to 115–150 words (don't just lift narration —
   a cut-down needs its own 0–3 s hook line).
2. Reuse the long-form's bible verbatim and its master/location images.
3. Regenerate the chosen beats in 9:16 (centre-safe framing) — cropping 16:9 exports
   loses the subject and the quality.
4. Same narrator take style, same trim discipline, captions on.
5. Pinned comment + end line point to the full documentary.

## Standalone teasers (next week's topic)
1. One verified hook fact from next week's early research (two sources, mini-brief).
2. Full payoff on that fact — then one line teasing the bigger story coming Tuesday.
3. These double as a topic test: a teaser that pops is evidence next week's long-form
   deserves its slot; a flat one is a cheap warning.
