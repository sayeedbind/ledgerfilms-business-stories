# Fact-check & legal — the rules that keep the channel alive

Business stories live on other people's reputations and real money figures. One invented
number or careless fraud claim can cost a strike, a lawsuit, or sponsor trust.

## Sourcing
- **Two independent sources** per load-bearing fact; at least one **primary** where one
  exists: SEC/Companies House filings, annual reports, earnings transcripts, court and
  regulator documents, official company histories.
- News (Reuters, Bloomberg, FT, WSJ, BBC) is a source; a competitor YouTube video is not.
  A Wikipedia claim is a lead — follow its citation, don't cite Wikipedia in the brief.
- Every figure in the research brief carries: value · year · source · URL. If any cell is
  empty, the figure does not enter the script.

## Language ladder for wrongdoing
Use the strongest word the evidence supports, and no stronger:
- **"was convicted of / pleaded guilty to"** — only with a court outcome.
- **"regulators found / the SEC said"** — attribute to the body, cite the document.
- **"was charged with / prosecutors alleged"** — allegations, clearly framed as such.
- **"reportedly / according to [outlet]"** — reported, not established.
Never state a living person's *motive* ("he wanted to fool investors") unless a court or
the person's own words establish it. Describe actions and documents instead.

## Numbers in narration
- Always with a year and a basis: "revenue peaked at $5.9 billion in 2004, according to
  Blockbuster's annual report" — not "they made billions".
- Round honestly ("about $6 billion"), never invent precision.
- Currency: state it (USD unless the company reported otherwise).

## Financial-advice boundary
- Explaining how something works = fine. Telling viewers what to buy, sell, or expect = banned.
- No price predictions, no "undervalued", no affiliate-linked product pushes in scripts.
- Every long-form description carries: *"LedgerFilms tells business stories for education
  and entertainment. This is not financial advice."*

## Other
- Real logos/trademarks: describe, don't generate. Model-rendered brand marks are both
  garbled and a trademark headache — brand colour + product shape carries the scene.
- Music and any third-party clips: licensed/library only, logged in the upload metadata.
- Corrections: if a published fact turns out wrong, pin a correction comment and fix the
  description the same day. Log it in the video's research brief.
