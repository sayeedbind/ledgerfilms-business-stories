# Niche & audience

**Channel:** LedgerFilmsHQ — https://www.youtube.com/@LedgerFilmsHQ
**Niche:** AI-Illustrated Business & Money Stories
**Viewer:** English-speaking, 18–45, US / UK / CA / AU. Two overlapping people:
the *self-improver* (wants lessons they can use) and the *curious professional*
(wants to understand how the business world actually works). Both are exactly who
finance, business-software and productivity advertisers pay a premium to reach —
program every creative choice for them, not for a general audience.

## Content pillars (rotate weekly)
1. **Rise & Fall** — Blockbuster, Kodak, Nokia, Toys "R" Us, WeWork. Built-in arc, built-in lesson.
2. **How They Actually Make Money** — Costco, IKEA, McDonald's (real estate), Ryanair, printers & razors.
3. **Founder Wars** — Adidas vs Puma, Coke vs Pepsi, Gates vs Jobs. Two bibles, one mechanism.
4. **Collapses & Scams** — Enron, Theranos, FTX, Wirecard. Highest curiosity, highest legal care.
5. **Money Mechanics** — credit-card rewards, index funds, franchising, subscriptions. Evergreen, safest for sponsors.

## House style (MagnatesMedia-inspired, not imitating)
- Faceless. One narrator. Cinematic AI-art scenes, teal-and-amber grade, film grain.
- Every video answers ONE question and reveals ONE money mechanism.
- Calm, confident narration — no shouting, no hype words ("insane", "crazy" banned in scripts).
- Numbers are the drama: state them plainly, with their year and source.
- End on a transferable observation about work/money. Never financial advice.

## What we never do
- Get-rich-quick framing, income promises, or "passive income" bait.
- Buy/sell calls, price predictions, crypto promotion.
- Unverifiable claims about living people; gossip presented as business history.
- Model-rendered logos, charts or readable text in scenes.
